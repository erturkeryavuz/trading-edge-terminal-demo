1. U.S. SEC EDGAR Public Filing Facts (Historical Snapshot: 2026-11-12)

Provider: U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC EDGAR — data.sec.gov).

Includes minimal normalized numeric GAAP accounting facts, derived ratios, filing dates, form types, and official EDGAR accession links for 17 U.S. operating companies plus non-operating ETF coverage metadata for SPY. Only raw factual numeric disclosures and filing metadata are extracted—no third-party filing prose narratives, exhibits, corporate logos, or personal executive records are bundled (note that 17 U.S.C. § 105 applies to U.S. federal government works rather than issuer-authored filing prose). Figures reflect a fixed historical snapshot retrieved on 2026-11-12 and are not refreshed live.

2. U.S. Department of the Treasury Par Yield Curves (2025-10-28 to 2026-11-10)

Provider: U.S. Department of the Treasury (Daily Treasury Par Yield Curve Rates).

Includes 13 maturity tenors (1M–30Y) for 2026-11-10 and 260 dated spread observations (from a 465-observation historical acquisition starting 2025-01-02). Compiled by U.S. federal officers and generally in the U.S. public domain (17 U.S.C. § 105), cited without implying Treasury endorsement.

3. Synthetic Bar-Revision & Breakout Replay Fixture

All 1-minute OHLCV bar values, duplicates, delayed revisions, and breakout evaluations in the Revision Replay workspace are 100% synthetic fabricated numbers (fixture:TEST) authored to verify point-in-time (as-of) anti-lookahead semantics. Session validation uses a bounded 2026–2027 NYSE Regular Trading Hours schedule (US-RTH-2026-2027/v1). These outputs are not real market prices and do not represent a validated or profitable trading strategy.
